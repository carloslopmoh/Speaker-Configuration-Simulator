# Sepaker-Configuration-Simulator
In my Bachelor’s thesis, a software has been developed that allows evaluating
the frequency response of a loudspeaker or set of loudspeakers in a given
space, taking into account the dimensions of that space and the positions of the
loudspeakers. To this end, concepts from signal processing, such as impulse response
and frequency response, wave propagation and acoustic engineering, will
be applied with the aim of creating a programme capable of accurately predicting
the acoustic behaviour of a speaker array, as well as individual speakers, such
as subwoofers. The software will enable the frequency response to be evaluated
at any point in the space, and will also generate various graphs to monitor its
behaviour, such as a heat map at a specific frequency or the variation of SPL with
regards to distance.
